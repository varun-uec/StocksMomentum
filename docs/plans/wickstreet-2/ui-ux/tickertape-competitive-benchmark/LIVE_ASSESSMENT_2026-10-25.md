# Wickstreet 2.0 vs Tickertape — Independent Live Benchmark

**Assessment date:** 25 October 2026 (IST) · **Scope:** mutual fund research journeys only
**Current verdict:** **4. Insufficient evidence for an overall verdict.**

## Executive verdict

The latest Wickstreet build has made visible progress: its current fund page puts six useful facts beside the historical NAV chart on desktop and exposes those six values on the first phone screen; the screener separates “show this value” from optional filtering; the fund benchmark page now shows AMFI Tier 1 TRI observations; and Compare puts decision answers above its chart. These were checked in fresh browser captures of the local application at about 00:10 IST on 25 October.

That evidence does **not** establish that Wickstreet is now the product an investor should prefer overall. The fresh Tickertape public session landed on a screener marketing page and a mutual-fund hub, not the interactive screener or PPFAS dossier needed for equivalent comparison. Some additional Tickertape dossier and authenticated-portfolio observations were made on 24 October, but they were not independently re-run on 25 October. Several required Wickstreet tasks also remain unverified in the fresh run: the live threshold result, the exact PPFAS/HDFC/Kotak comparison, holdings overlap for that set, current SIP/SWP calculations, dark-theme quality across key routes, and advanced-filter calculations.

Accordingly, this review chooses **“Insufficient evidence for an overall verdict,”** not because both products are equal, but because key matched tasks were not completed on both current builds. The strongest current evidence points to Wickstreet's advantage in explainable historical risk and decision-first comparison, and Tickertape's advantage in its familiar fund hub, fund peer discovery and previously observed signed-in portfolio analysis. Tickertape also had a previously observed PPFAS 1Y return inconsistency; that error is evidence against its trust, but does not establish Wickstreet's overall superiority.

The local app answered requests during the independent capture, but a later direct check during this assessment found `http://localhost:8000/mf` unavailable (connection refused). Docker access was denied by the execution sandbox, so the app was not restarted. This operational availability issue is recorded separately from product UX, and no application code or data was changed.

### Design defects vs functional gaps vs data constraints vs assumptions

- **Design defects:** clipped desktop metric column; sticky phone overlays; faint source dates lost on phone; subdued consistency in a few dense screens. These are visible defects, not speculative preferences.
- **Functional gaps/unverified integrations:** current threshold result count; exact peer comparison route; CAS-to-analysis completion; current scheme-to-scheme holdings overlap; fresh Advanced filter and calculator acceptance.
- **Data constraints:** holdings month differs by disclosure cadence; not every scheme/month has a stored portfolio; official AMFI benchmark series is now present for the reviewed PPFAS scheme but coverage for all schemes was not tested. Continue showing explicit missingness rather than proxies presented as facts.
- **Unverified assumptions:** Tickertape’s current post-CTA screener features, premium gating and calculations; how much its current authenticated portfolio offers free users; comparative task times; beginner preference; current dark-theme parity; whether users interpret scorecards as suitability. Do not convert these into competitive claims without fresh tests.
