## 1. B0 decision to resolve

**Approved strategic direction for B0 planning:** build Wickstreet 2.0 as a dedicated mutual-fund research and analytics product comparable in useful MF capabilities to Tickertape's MF offering. “Tickertape for mutual funds” is internal competitive shorthand only, not a public-facing brand, affiliation or endorsement claim. Wickstreet uses its own product, methods, code and lawful data sources. The goal is meaningful capability parity over time, staged by customer value, rights, regulation, cost and delivery capacity. Wickstreet 2.0 remains MF-first; it does not recreate Tickertape's stock, US-market or full multi-asset ecosystem, and it does not disturb Wickstreet 3.0 or Momentum25.

B0 must determine which economically viable MF capability subset should come first and whether the ₹199/year offer can support it. A paid research workspace is the leading hypothesis, not the selected product or approved bundle. Test H1 (value from useful MF capability parity at a much lower price) and H2 (a simpler, clearer and reproducible research experience with less unnecessary effort) through one shared research workstation, not two separate products. Tickertape already connects research activities; integration alone is not a demonstrated advantage. The long-term product direction is set; these are related value questions, not alternatives to the Tickertape-for-MF ambition. B0 must also establish whether required data, licences and provider integrations fit a viable rights-and-cost envelope.

The product job under consideration is: **help self-directed investors understand trade-offs among mutual funds they are already considering and explore the consequences of their own choices.** Wickstreet would calculate and explain; the user chooses funds, amounts, allocations, assumptions and scenarios. Wickstreet does not prescribe an investment, allocation, return assumption or action.

The product direction is accepted as the planning baseline; the first-release scope, exact Pro bundle and willingness to pay are not validated or approved. Wickstreet need not invent a category or reject useful features just because another service offers them. Comparable functionality may be valuable at a lower price; integrated usability and transparent, reproducible analysis may be additional advantages. The previous fixed price-intent threshold of 10 of 50 participants is withdrawn: it was not derived from a defined audience, economic hurdle or statistical design.

Treat B0 as three separate decision gates, rather than requiring commercial proof before a small synthetic demonstration:

- **Discovery Gate:** proceed to a prototype request if user tasks, priority and a testable first slice are credible. Full data rights, legal clearance for commercial publication and paid-intent evidence are not prerequisites for this gate.
- **Prototype Gate:** separately authorize one small synthetic, non-production demonstration to measure whether the shared research workflow improves task outcomes and whether H1/H2 merit further work. This is not B1 authority or commercial readiness.
- **Separate B1 Fixture-Engineering Decision:** after prototype evidence, the owner may separately authorize bounded fixture-only domain engineering if the evidence justifies it. Commercial readiness is not a prerequisite for this synthetic work; it grants no live-data, customer, paid-feature or launch authority.
- **Commercial Gate:** required before live or paid customer exposure, after price evidence, data rights and costs, feature-specific regulatory review, security/privacy/operations and lean economics support the exact proposed bundle. This gate does not retroactively block earlier approved synthetic work.

Separately authorized internal B2–B5 preparation may establish the evidence for that Commercial Gate. Source/use permissions must precede affected ingestion, and appropriate safeguards apply to any permitted internal data handling. Customer accounts, payment collection and live/paid exposure do not follow from preparatory authority. Production tenancy and commercial publication clearance are not prerequisites for an approved synthetic test with appropriate participant safeguards.

At any gate, pivot or defer if the evidence does not support continuing. A positive gate result requests the next owner decision; it does not automatically grant it. A no-charge price-intent action does not prove purchase, conversion or renewal.

## 2. Customer problem hypothesis

**Primary hypothesis segment:** Indian retail investors who make their own mutual-fund decisions and have recently compared schemes or planned contributions toward a goal. This is unvalidated.

Initial discovery should investigate real recent behavior before describing Wickstreet or naming candidate features:

1. A self-directed investor shortlisted two or more schemes and had to reconcile facts, performance periods, risks, costs or holdings across several tools.
2. An investor considered changing an existing choice or comparing alternative user-selected allocations and could not understand how assumptions or periods changed the result.
3. An investor wanted to revisit a prior comparison and remember the schemes, assumptions, dates and rationale used.

These are discovery prompts, not evidence that the problems occur or that people will pay to solve them. Interviews should ask participants to reconstruct a specific recent decision, the tools and steps used, friction encountered, what they could not conclude, and what they did next. Do not begin by pitching calculators or leading participants toward the proposed solution.

Requests for fund selection, risk profiling, a recommended allocation or advice on what to buy are not validation of the self-service product. Record those needs as possible segment mismatch and negative evidence rather than discarding otherwise eligible interviews. Personalised Guidance remains separately gated.

### 3.4 Benchmark protocol

Measure parity by user outcomes on comparable MF tasks, not by matching a feature or filter count. The [Tickertape MF screener](https://tickertape.in/screener/mutual-fund) advertises 60+ screening filters and custom/prebuilt screens; its [screener guide](https://www.tickertape.in/blog/how-to-use-tickertape-mutual-fund-screener/) describes saving screens. A reported screener-note option remains unverified; the owner-authorized session verified saving/reopening but did not verify note support. Saved research alone is therefore not a presumed differentiator. Use one shared MF research workstation to test H1 (whether a useful, cohesive subset of Tickertape-class MF capabilities is worth ₹199/year) and H2 (whether integrating discovery, method-consistent comparison, user-directed scenarios and reproducible research history improves outcomes further). Compare task completion, accuracy/comprehension, time and effort, consistency across periods, ability to explain assumptions and limitations, provenance visibility, and ability to reproduce or revisit a prior analysis. Define the benchmark protocol during Discovery; compare with a Wickstreet demonstration only after separate Prototype Gate approval. Do not require Wickstreet to outperform Tickertape on every task for H1 to pass; do not infer parity from raw feature counts. Respect service terms; no scraping, account circumvention, copying or reverse engineering. Any further hands-on use or paid subscription requires separate owner approval and must comply with current terms. Public desk claims are not a substitute for an authorized task benchmark.

## 5. Regulatory boundary for research and analytics

Removing personalization reduces one source of Investment Adviser (IA) risk; it does not create a categorical exemption. Paid fund-level analysis, reports, rankings, scores, screening order, alerts or statements that influence an investment decision may require assessment under the SEBI Research Analyst (RA) framework as well as the IA framework. Product labels and disclaimers do not decide classification.

The current reference set includes SEBI's [Investment Adviser Master Circular dated 6 February 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/feb-2026/master-circular-for-investment-advisers_99569.html), [Research Analyst Master Circular dated 6 February 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/feb-2026/master-circular-for-research-analysts_99571.html), [RA FAQs dated 23 July 2025](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jul-2025/frequently-asked-questions-faqs-related-to-regulatory-provisions-for-research-analysts_95549.html), and the [RA Regulations consolidated as last amended 10 October 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/oct-2026/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-research-analysts-regulations-2014-last-amended-on-october-10-2026-_105294.html). The [RA Amendment Regulations, 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/oct-2026/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-research-analysts-amendment-regulations-2026_105155.html) provide that call-recording requirements for communications with institutional-investor clients are not mandatory and commence on the thirtieth day after Official Gazette publication. The amendment does not establish an exemption for retail analytics; verify the Gazette publication date for its exact effective date before relying on it.

The following is a **provisional product-risk screen, not legal advice or a safe-harbour determination**:

| Workflow or feature | Provisional concern | Required control before publication |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic using only user-entered values, user-chosen funds and scenarios, with no Wickstreet selection or ranking | Lower IA/RA risk than advice or research recommendations, but classification depends on context, consideration, marketing and how the tool frames its results. | Keep inputs user-controlled; explain the calculation and assumptions; avoid default investments/returns and preference signals. Obtain legal review of the actual flow and price model. |
| Fund-level historical return, risk, downside, cost or comparative analysis sold as Pro | May constitute paid research/analysis concerning securities or be used as a basis for investment decisions; non-personalization alone does not resolve RA scope. | Counsel must classify each metric, comparison, report and distribution method under current RA/IA rules before it is offered. Validate methods and rights separately. |
| Ranking, score, “best” list, suggested shortlist, default ordering, alert or nudge about a scheme | Elevated RA concern; wording, methodology and presentation can turn descriptive information into a recommendation or opinion. Personalized use may also raise IA issues. | Exclude from launch candidate until feature-specific legal review and any registration/operating requirements are resolved. Do not assume a “not advice” label cures the design. |
| User-specific allocation, fund selection, suitability, rebalance instruction or amount recommendation | Strong potential IA concern because the product is tailoring investment advice or portfolio guidance; may also involve securities research. | Keep in future Personalised Guidance scope, separately gated for regulatory, operational and commercial approval. |
| Email/report/export or notification containing fund analysis | The medium does not remove RA/IA concerns; reports or recurring communications can carry the same analysis/recommendation. | Review the precise generated content, recipients, timing and workflow before enabling it. |

The familiar distinction between factual data, general research and personalized advice must be applied to Wickstreet's actual paid screens, algorithms, default states, marketing, reports and notifications. A generic disclaimer is not a substitute for that assessment. No IA registration, KYC or advisory agreement is assumed for a genuinely self-directed workflow; require them only if feature-specific legal analysis establishes the obligation. Ordinary SaaS account, privacy and payment obligations remain separate.

**Regulatory publication gate:** before any live user-facing fund metric, comparative report, ranking, score, alert or paid research workflow is published, obtain a written feature-by-feature IA/RA classification based on final UX, methodology, consideration model and marketing. If the classification is uncertain, hold the affected feature. Do not use user testing as authority to publish it.

## 6. Data rights and ₹199 cost envelope

No supplier, licence, price, or right to commercially display, derive, store, cache, or redistribute data is assumed. Competitor claims about their own data sources do not establish rights Wickstreet has. Public availability or access through an API is not itself permission for commercial republication; see the prior [D0-R source research](12-d0r-data-source-research.md).

| Candidate use | Minimum data | Rights/cost questions before the promise is made | Fallback if not viable |
|---|---|---|---|
| User-entered formula scenarios | No market feed | No third-party market feed needed for pure arithmetic; review product wording and input privacy. | Synthetic examples and user inputs only. |
| Scheme discovery and factual comparison | Scheme identity, status, category, plan/option and displayed facts | Automated retrieval, storage, correction, display, comparison, attribution, caching, backups, reports, subscribers, volume tiers, term and deletion. | Narrow or omit unlicensed fields/catalog. |
| Historical NAV charts, returns, risk or SIP replay | Dated NAV observations, corrections and coverage | Rights for historical retention, derived metrics, subscriber display, scenario replay, exports, cache/backups and commercial scale. | No NAV-dependent Pro claim or fund-specific historical result. |
| Holdings, overlap or portfolio analysis | Dated scheme holdings, identifiers and weights | Per-source rights to ingest, retain, derive overlap and display cross-scheme analysis. | Defer; not a prerequisite for initial problem discovery. |

Establish a preliminary affordability envelope early in Discovery, before the team becomes committed to a data-heavy parity proposition. At an **illustrative** ₹199 annual price inclusive of an assumed 18% GST, the price excluding that tax is ₹199 / 1.18 = **₹168.64 per paying subscriber/year**, before payment processing, support, acquisition, hosting, data licensing and other costs. This is a sensitivity calculation, not confirmation of Wickstreet's GST classification, rate, input-tax-credit treatment or net receipts; confirm those with a qualified tax professional before pricing or launch. The [CBIC sectoral FAQ](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/sectoral-faq.html) states an 18% rate for IT services, but does not determine Wickstreet's specific classification.

Use the initial ceiling to estimate a maximum affordable data-cost range per paying subscriber, not to assume that the full ₹168.64 is available for data. Subtract payment fees, customer support and service costs, acquisition/renewal costs, and a chosen operating contribution; also allocate free-tier data and traffic costs across the paying base using explicit free-active-users-per-payer, usage, conversion and retention assumptions. Then test the range against supplier minimums and volume tiers. Refine it as actual usage, conversion and quotations become available.

Before accepting any data-dependent Pro promise, establish a viable **rights-and-cost envelope** using written quotations or other verifiable terms: minimum useful scheme universe/history, refresh and quality commitments, permitted derived uses/displays, price and volume tiers, attribution, support, renewal, termination, deletion and backup treatment. Model the cost at lean-launch and plausible growth subscriber volumes against net subscription receipts after GST, payment costs, support, acquisition, free-tier servicing and founder effort. Do not enter an unquoted licence as ₹0 or treat startup credits as a data-rights solution. If the rights and cost are unknown, the B0 result can support only a user-input/synthetic-data prototype and cannot validate a live-data paid promise. Rights must be established before ingesting or publishing the affected commercial datasets.

## 7. Validation sequence and gates

No customer interviews or product research have yet been conducted for this revised proposition. The sequence below is proposed and **does not authorize contacting participants, collecting personal information, recording sessions, implementing a prototype, purchasing data or spending**. Each activity requires its own approval and operating/privacy procedures.

### 7.1 Three separate B0 gates

The gates prevent a circular dependency: a small synthetic demonstration may be useful evidence for selecting a first release, but it must be specifically authorized and is not B1 or commercial launch approval.

1. **B0 Discovery Gate:** conduct owner-approved qualitative discovery and competitor task benchmarking; identify recurring MF research tasks, rank user problems, compare public/observed alternatives, draft one focused first-release proposition and identify the data, regulatory and cost questions it raises. Prepare the H1/H2 protocol in one shared workstation: compare equivalent tasks in a basic parity experience and an integrated-workflow experience; specify the outcome measures, what improvement would count as meaningful, and how order effects will be reduced (counterbalance task/experience order where practical). The integrated experience is tested for incremental value; this does not require two separate products. Establish the preliminary data-cost envelope in §6 using explicit tax, free-to-paid, usage and retention assumptions. A prototype request must identify one prioritized investor task, evidence of its frequency or severity, a competitor gap or viable parity opportunity, and a narrow synthetic brief with matched information and a predefined outcome. No full statistical price evidence, live-data clearance or commercial legal clearance is required to request a synthetic prototype.
2. **B0 Prototype Gate:** seek separate, explicit owner approval for a small synthetic-data, non-production demonstration of that shared workflow. Define tasks and success measures before building it. Use clearly fictitious or conspicuously synthetic scheme fixtures; no live NAV, market-data-derived facts, customer accounts, payment, production persistence, regulated feature publication, vendor integration or commercial launch. Participant recruitment, observation, consent and personal-data/recording handling still require separate approval and suitable safeguards. The prototype compares task outcomes and H1/H2 value; it is not B1 authority.
3. **B0 Commercial Gate:** before any live or paid customer exposure, present the fixed first-release bundle and proposed price, price-intent evidence designed for the reachable audience and lean economics, written data permissions and costs for each dependent feature, feature-specific IA/RA and other legal review, security/privacy/operating readiness, and a reconciled launch economics case. ## 8. Deterministic calculator and scenario method (proposal)

These formula conventions remain a starting methodology proposal. The previous review found the formulas sound as a starting point, but final precision, rounding, bounds and examples remain owner decisions. No calculator implementation is authorized.

### 8.1 Shared rules

- The user chooses the amount/contribution, duration, assumed annual effective return, scheme labels and scenario set. Do not prefill an investment amount, fund, return, risk category or allocation. No result is shown until required inputs are valid.
- Treat an entered return as a hypothetical effective annual assumption, not a forecast or fund return. Convert it to the equivalent monthly rate: `i = (1 + r)^(1/12) − 1`, where `r > −1`.
- Use end-of-month SIP contributions in formula mode and show the timing convention. Calendar-date NAV execution is a separate, rights-gated historical simulation.
- Preserve calculation precision and round only for display under a finally approved rule. Show inputs, periods, method version and limitations. Identical inputs and method version produce identical results.
- Describe outputs as “hypothetical illustration”, “assumed return” and “contribution implied by your inputs.” Do not use “best,” “expected return,” “profit” or “recommended contribution.”
- Base formula mode excludes taxes, exit loads, stamp duty, expense ratios, inflation and transaction timing. Show exclusions; do not silently insert guessed costs.
- Preserve user-selected scenario ordering. Do not declare a winner, rank suitability or label an outcome recommended.

### 8.2 Candidate formulas

Let `P` be the monthly contribution, `A` the initial lump sum, `G` the target amount, `n` the number of monthly periods, and `i` the monthly rate derived from the user's assumption.

| Calculator | User inputs | Candidate result |
|---|---|---|
| SIP illustration | `P`, `n`, annual effective assumption `r` | Contributions `P × n`; end-of-month illustrative value `P × ((1+i)^n − 1) / i`; if `i = 0`, value `P × n`. |
| Lumpsum illustration | `A`, `n`, annual effective assumption `r` | Illustrative value `A × (1+i)^n`; show difference from initial amount separately as an illustration. |
| Goal contribution | `G`, `n`, annual effective assumption `r` | Required month-end contribution `G × i / ((1+i)^n − 1)`; if `i = 0`, `G / n`. |
| Scenario comparison | Two or more independently entered cases | Display inputs, contributions and illustrative values side by side in user-selected order. |

Annual `r` must be above −100% for the conversion. Proposed accepted bounds for initial discussion only: monthly contribution ₹1–₹10,00,000; lumpsum ₹1–₹1,00,00,000; goal ₹1–₹10,00,00,000; 1–600 whole months; annual effective assumption −99% to +100% in 0.01 percentage-point increments. These are not approved product limits. Invalid inputs should be explained, not silently clamped.

Proposed calculation implementation contract (also pending approval): decimal arithmetic with at least 34 significant digits internally; pin a method identifier; round illustrative values and differences to whole rupees using ROUND_HALF_UP; show total contributions exactly; round required goal contributions upward to whole rupees. Explicitly test zero rate, permitted negative rates, minimum/maximum values and periods, one month, maximum term, invalid inputs and goal rounding. No binary floating-point for money inputs or intermediate currency amounts.

Historical SIP outcomes on real NAVs are a different mode. Before proposing it for Pro, specify NAV corrections/missing dates, plan and option behavior, contribution-to-NAV timing, unit precision, fees/taxes/distributions, reconciliation examples, output caveats, data rights and full cost. It is excluded from a no-data prototype.
