# Wickstreet 2.0 Stage B — Implementation Plan

> **Status: Accepted as the Stage B planning baseline (2026-10-22).** The owner approved this delivery sequence. It remains a plan only: it does not authorize code changes, migrations, dependency additions, data acquisition or ingestion, provider selection, infrastructure expenditure, deployment, commits or pushes. Each implementation slice and any associated spending requires separate owner authority.
>
> **Authority:** [Accepted ADD Amendment A3](../../architecture/ADD-amendment-A3.md) is authoritative for architecture, product boundaries and capability preservation. The [canonical Wickstreet 2.0/3.0 release plan](13-wickstreet-2.0-mf-first-release-plan.md) governs product scope, data-rights requirements and bootstrap economics. This document adds sequencing detail only; it does not supersede either.

## 1. Stage B outcome

Stage B is the smallest authenticated MF SaaS release that can test whether customers value useful mutual-fund research. It comprises factual discovery and comparison over data Wickstreet is permitted to use, supported by basic SIP, lumpsum and goal calculations whose inputs are chosen by the user. The Free/Pro boundaries are provisional: a paid research workspace is the leading B0 hypothesis, not a selected product; basic calculators are free/supporting candidates, not the assumed paid differentiator. Exact analytical depth, filters, persistence and paywalls depend on customer-task, benchmark and rights/cost evidence in the [B0 specification](15-stage-b0-product-and-value-specification.md#4-candidate-free-and-pro-proposition). CAS import, linked portfolio analysis, broker connectivity and personalised allocation guidance are outside Stage B.

Deliver the research and calculator experience in independently reviewable slices. Begin with synthetic fixtures on existing development infrastructure. Real MF data may enter only after written permission for the exact retrieval, storage, display, calculation, derived-output and export uses has been verified and separately authorized. If those rights cannot be secured economically, narrow or defer the affected market-data features; do not substitute an unverified scraper or public-site download.

The first user research and prototype can precede production identity, payments and market data. Before an account, payment, saved private scenario or other customer-specific record is exposed, the applicable identity, tenant isolation, privacy, security, recovery and payment controls must be in place. Stage B does not require CAS or advisory KYC.

**Product boundary:** users choose the funds, amounts, allocations, dates and assumptions. Wickstreet reports calculated consequences and factual comparisons. No platform-selected portfolio, profile-driven defaults, recommendation, suitability assessment, ranking presented as advice, or transaction instruction is part of Stage B. Personalised Guidance remains a future capability with its own regulatory, commercial and owner-approval gate.

**Capability Preservation Invariant:** All existing Momentum25 and Wickstreet stock-market capabilities, including their domain engines, APIs, persistence, charting, strategies, research, screening, backtesting and saved workspaces, must be preserved. The Wickstreet 2.0 MF-first release may hide or defer their exposure in the commercial interface, but must not delete, disable, regress or irreversibly alter these capabilities. Any future replacement or deprecation requires explicit owner approval and regression verification.

## 4. Proposed module ownership and interfaces

Keep Stage B inside the modular monolith. Each module owns its application contract and persistence; other modules call that contract instead of importing internal ORM models or querying its tables.

| Module | Owns | Stage B interfaces and dependencies | First release boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity and tenancy | External identity mapping, local principal, tenant, membership, role and lifecycle. | OIDC callback/session adapter supplies a verified principal; application authorization resolves tenant on the server. It may be called by billing and user-state modules. | No client-supplied tenant IDs as authority. Do not write new public portfolio-like records until enforcement is tested. |
| Subscription and entitlements | Product/price identifiers, subscription state, event receipts and feature entitlement. | Payment-provider adapter accepts verified, idempotent events and exposes a small `entitlement_for(principal, capability)` application query. Depends on identity, not on MF internals. | No secrets or provider payloads in browser trust. No entitlement granted from redirect/query parameters. Trial/free policy remains an owner decision. |
| MF reference data and rights | Scheme identifiers, scheme/plan/option/category facts, sources, permission evidence, effective uses and data release status. | Read contract for permitted scheme facts; ingestion port accepts normalized records with provenance and rights reference. Analytics reads only admitted data. | Quarantine/unapproved status fails closed. Do not reuse `Security` as a fund entity. |
| MF NAV and analytics | NAV observations and versioned factual metrics, coverage and calculation metadata. | Read-only query contract for scheme NAV series and metric snapshots. Depends on admitted reference/NAV data and approved method registry. | Stage B only publishes covered fields and periods with rights. No synthetic fixture leaks into production. |
| Simulation | Pure, deterministic calculators over explicit user inputs and, only where allowed, selected MF data. | Versioned request/result contract. Prefer synchronous calculation for the bounded basic calculators; persist only through the scenario module after explicit user action. | Domain functions independent of FastAPI, React, SQLAlchemy and payment provider. No optimization/advice service. |
| User scenarios and saved research | Tenant-owned saved inputs, labels and report references. | Tenant-scoped CRUD port; delegates entitlement check and calls simulation/MF read interfaces. | May be postponed if pilot demand does not require persistence. |
| Existing equity/Momentum25 | Existing security, market data, strategy, run, research, chart and workspace domains. | Existing APIs and modules remain owners of their records. The MF UI may link to existing chart/workstation routes but must not bind MF data to equity internals. | No mandatory refactor or endpoint change for Stage B. |

**Dependency direction:** identity/tenancy and rights admission are foundational. Subscription asks identity for a principal and serves entitlements to application use cases. MF ingestion writes through MF-owned contracts. MF analytics reads MF-owned admitted data. Simulation calls calculation functions and, for explicitly selected historical NAV simulations, the MF analytics read contract. Web/API layers call application interfaces. No module imports another module's ORM model. No cross-context transaction is needed for a stateless calculation. Saving a scenario is a transaction owned by the scenario module; a payment event is an idempotent billing transaction and entitlement is derived from its normalized subscription state.

Provider calls are outside user-facing database transactions. Verify a webhook signature before recording the event; record its provider event ID and process idempotently. Avoid a distributed transaction across payment and application data. Identity callback state/nonce and session creation are handled by the authentication adapter. Stage B needs no event bus: simple synchronous application calls and a bounded scheduled importer (only if source terms require it) are sufficient.

## 5. Data acquisition and permissions gate

The plan does not select or buy a data provider. D0-R documents material unresolved MF data-rights gaps, including AMFI terms; source and field availability cannot be inferred from web access or from the existing stock-data provider.

For each proposed dataset/source, owner review must record:

1. Source, contracting entity, quotation or written permission, effective term and permitted user population.
2. Rights for automated retrieval, retention/storage, derived metrics, historical simulation, commercial subscriber display, screenshots/reports, exports, cache copies, backups and deletion at termination.
3. Coverage, update cadence, corrections, identifiers, redistribution restrictions, attribution/lag and service limits.
4. Cost trigger (fixed, volume, display or subscriber band), GST/settlement terms, renewal and exit/data deletion obligations.
5. A documented feature-to-permission mapping and an automated or operational publish gate that fails closed when the permission is absent, expired or outside scope.

Synthetic fixtures are allowed in development and tests. User-entered return assumptions can power assumption-only calculators without a market-data feed. Historical fund calculations, current NAV display and fund discovery each need their own rights and method check. Educational price-data permission, if relied on, must be reviewed for actual source, recipient, delay, agreement and permitted use; it is not treated as commercial SaaS licensing by default.

If the only economically viable offer before rights is an assumption-only calculator, validate whether customers would pay for it before starting a paid launch. Do not represent synthetic or stale data as current. No data request, licence purchase, scraping, import or publication is authorized by this plan.

## 7. Development, production and operational dependencies

### Development (₹0 incremental hosting target)

- Use existing workstation, Docker Compose and synthetic fixtures. No production customer data, production secrets or paid always-on hosting.
- Retain existing Python/FastAPI, React/TypeScript/Vite, PostgreSQL, Alembic, OpenAPI generation, pytest, mypy, Ruff, `lint-imports`, Vitest and Playwright conventions. Add a package only when a specific approved slice needs it; document its license, maintenance, security surface and removal path.
- Keep Redis optional for new MF work. The basic deterministic calculators run in-process. A separate worker, queue or new scheduler is unnecessary until a measurable job/runtime or isolation requirement exists; CAS remains Stage C and requires its A3 isolation design.
- Intern work, if used, is optional capacity and limited to lawful supervised engagements on synthetic fixtures. Founder retains identity, tenancy, billing, permissions, security, secrets, production, migration and release ownership. No critical path depends on intern availability.

### Initial SaaS (costs only when exposure requires them)

Expected dependencies to quote and approve include an OIDC provider, payment provider, production host/network/TLS, database and encrypted off-host backup, email or support delivery, and any commercially licensed MF dataset. Costs remain **unknown**, not zero, until actual quotes and terms are reviewed. Do not assume startup credits, free-tier adequacy, a specific provider, merchant acceptance or a ₹0 data licence. Choose a small deployment and scale by measured traffic, storage, query/job load, support and legal obligation.

Before any production account/payment exposure, agree the applicable entity, privacy notice and user rights, account deletion/retention, security incident contact, refund/renewal handling, applicable business/tax filings, data-controller/processor roles, and operational recovery targets. Obtain proportionate targeted advice or review where workflow or jurisdiction creates uncertainty; do not infer an enterprise annual retainer from this plan. No spend is authorized here.

### Measurement before scaling

Record a baseline for the actual query mix and pilot data volume: response latency, error rate, PostgreSQL pool wait/query plans, CPU/memory, disk and backup size, import duration/staleness, calculator runtime, payment-webhook failures and founder support hours. Run synthetic ramp, burst and soak tests at owner-selected levels. A3's example request rates and concurrency remain scenarios, not launch thresholds. Scale a component only after an observed user impact, SLO miss, capacity constraint, data-rights requirement or operational risk has an owner and costed remedy. Subscriber count alone is not a trigger.

## 8. Security, verification and release controls

- Derive tenant scope only from verified server-side identity. Apply authorization to every row read, mutation, export, background job and object reference; reject client-provided tenant scope. Add cross-tenant negative tests for each private-data path.
- Before SaaS exposure, inventory API/SPA routes, administrative/import endpoints and jobs with their intended audience, authorization, tenant/data scope and entitlement requirements. Hiding stock navigation is not API isolation. Legacy global/private paths must not become reachable through the commercial surface without appropriate controls; preserve the complete owner/local stock application, APIs and records. Verify anonymous, wrong-tenant and insufficient-entitlement denials, including reads, writes, exports and error responses that could reveal another user's state.
- Verify tenant-aware keys, uniqueness and repository queries for new private records; a nullable `tenant_id` or login dependency alone is insufficient. Do not silently assign legacy global workspaces/watchlists to new customers. Use additive MF persistence where appropriate and review any legacy migration separately under the preservation invariant.
- Keep shared permitted MF reference data separate from tenant-private scenarios. Public/read-only routes still enforce dataset publish status, data freshness and user-visible attribution/limitations.
- Use provider-managed OIDC credentials and payment secrets only server-side. Validate issuer, audience, signature, nonce/state and callback origin; protect session cookies, CSRF-sensitive operations, account enumeration and webhook replay. Choose rate limits from measured abuse/load risk and external-provider limits.
- Do not store payment-card data. Store the minimum normalized transaction/subscription references needed for entitlements and reconciliation. Define deletion/retention and export behavior for account and scenario data before collection.
- Record simulator method version, explicit user input and any permitted data revision/cutoff. Deterministic replays must match; missing/stale source data is visible rather than silently imputed. Do not include access tokens, private scenario contents or payment secrets in logs.
- Required proposed checks per slice: backend unit/integration tests, schema migrations on a fresh database, migration drift check, `lint-imports`, API/OpenAPI type generation review, frontend unit/E2E tests, tenant isolation tests once B4 exists, and regression verification for all preserved equity capabilities. Test scripts are part of future authorized implementation, not work performed by this planning document.
- Before shared schema, identity, routing or persistence changes ship, test upgrades from representative existing-schema fixtures as well as an empty database. Check preserved strategy identities including `strategy_id=30`, stored manifests, historical run records and owner workspaces. MF-only imports/corrections must leave existing stock manifest identities and deterministic replay outputs unchanged; new MF tables must not silently join the stock revision registry. Fixtures and approved historical evidence must respect the sealed hold-out.
- Record passing critical Momentum25/stock journeys: screening/rank/API output; chart/workspace save and reopen, drawings and alerts; strategy configuration and research/backtest execution; and saved screens. Run the relevant existing Playwright journeys and backend contract/regression checks in CI or a recorded release gate; generic CI success or tier-classification golden tests alone are not proof of end-to-end preservation. Reproduce the review's code-level risks against the then-current code before choosing a fix; no test or CI change is implemented by this document.
- Stage B recovery gate: encrypted backup stored off-host and restore rehearsed against a clean target; define retention/expiry for backups and the practical deletion model. Point-in-time recovery or replicas are future options if measured recovery needs justify added operations/cost.

No Stage B activity handles CAS files. Any later CAS slice must use A3's dedicated bounded parser environment with no unrestricted or public internet egress, only explicitly allowlisted private endpoints, and no general application database credentials.

## 9. Risks and stop conditions

| Risk | Response / stop condition |
|---|---|
| No commercial MF source can be secured at a price compatible with ₹199/year | Stop commercial-data publication; test an assumption-only product's demand and economics. Do not ingest data under personal/educational terms. |
| Research scope or UI drifts into advice | Remove the behavior or stop that slice; conduct actual-workflow classification. Personalised Guidance needs separate approval. |
| Legacy anonymous/global APIs leak tenant data | Do not expose those paths to SaaS. Add/replace scoped application contracts and prove isolation before account-linked use. |
| Payment or identity costs exceed unit economics or provider rejects the business | Do not launch paid subscriptions; compare alternatives with actual quotes and verify permitted collection methods before selecting. |
| Broad data catalog causes cost and methodology sprawl | Start from one owner-approved source/use case; add datasets/metrics only with rights, quality and demand evidence. |
| New MF code changes equity semantics or removes stock access | Stop release; restore additive boundary and pass Capability Preservation regression gate. |
| Founder support or incident load exceeds sustainable capacity | Cap pilot intake, simplify scope or pause onboarding until support capacity is restored; do not automatically add salaried/SRE spend. |
| Intern engagement or IP terms are not valid | Do not grant repo access until engagement is reviewed and signed; product schedule cannot depend on intern work. |
